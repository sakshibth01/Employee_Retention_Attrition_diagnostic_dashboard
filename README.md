# Employee Retention & Attrition Diagnostic Dashboard
An interactive Power BI diagnostic dashboard designed to analyze workforce attrition patterns, departmental turnover rates, demographic correlations, and salary impacts to support strategic HR retention planning.

## Dashboard Preview 
[Employee Retention Dashboard Preview](Employee-Retention-Attrition-Diagnostic-Dashboard.png)

## Key Metrics & Features
**KPI Summary Cards: **Instant executive visibility into **Total Employees (1K)**, **Attrition Rate (14.20%)**, **Attrition Count (142)**, and **Average Salary (87.85)**.
**Attrition Rate by Job Role: **Horizontal bar ranking highlighting highest turnover roles (*Executive: 19.50%, Associate: 16.25%, Manager: 15.03%).
**Departmental Breakdown: **Donut chart analyzing attrition volume distribution across *Finance, Human Resources, Sales, Research & Development, and Marketing*.
**Demographic Diagnostic: **100% Stacked Column Chart detailing attrition trends by **Age Group** (*Senior, Mid-level, junior*) and **Gender**.
**Dynamic Slicers: **Interactive filters for **OverTime** Status (*Yes/No) and **Department** selection.

##  Key Business Insights
**Role-Specific Attrition Risk: **Executive and Associate roles experience the highest turnover rates, indicating a need for target retention incentive and career pathing.
**Overtime Impact: **Evaluating overtime status against attrition reveals critical stress-points impacting employee retention.
**Targeted Demographics: **Senior (41+) and Mid-level (28-40) age groups demonstrate specific retention dynamics across male and female cohorts.


## Tools Used
**Power BI desktop** (Data Modelling, Visualizations, Dashboard Layout)
**Power Query** (Data Cleaning, Transformation & Age Grouping)
**DAX** (Custom Measures for Attrition Rate & Metric Aggregations)
